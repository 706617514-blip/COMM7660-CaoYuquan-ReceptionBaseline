# Asset inventory

Required by the brief's Table 8 (`product/` and `media/`: "necessary original/licensed media;
asset inventory"). This file records every asset the product ships and where it came from.

## The product ships no image, audio or video files

Emberfield generates all of its art at runtime from Phaser Graphics primitives. There are no
PNG, JPEG, SVG, audio or video files in `game/`. This is a deliberate choice with two
consequences worth stating:

- **Nothing can 404.** A missing asset is the most common way a static prototype breaks on
  someone else's machine, and there are no assets to miss.
- **The look is plain.** The brief states that visual polish alone earns no marks, so the
  effort went into mechanics and testability instead. The result is legible but not attractive,
  and that is a known limitation rather than an oversight.

## Data

No licensed or restricted media is used. The Steam records in `data/` are public API responses;
attribution and access conditions are in `data/source-access-manifest.md`. No reviewer
identifiers, avatars or review text are stored.

## What is not here

No fonts are bundled: the page uses system font stacks. No audio: `audio: { noAudio: true }` is
set deliberately, to avoid autoplay restrictions and to keep the asset count at zero.

# Recruitment message

Ready to send. Copy, fill the three brackets, and send to individuals rather than to a
group — a mass post gets volunteers who are curious about you, not about the product, and
those sessions produce polite feedback instead of usable evidence.

**Send this first, before building anything else.** Recruiting is the longest-lead item in
Phase 2: people need to reply, and then you need to find a slot. Building features while
waiting is the correct order; starting recruitment after the build is how the pilot ends up
with two participants.

---

## Message to individuals

> Subject: 15 minutes — would you try a tool about Steam review scores?
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm building a small tool for the COMM7660 project and I need a handful of people to try
> it for about 15 minutes. I'm asking you because [you've shipped something on Steam /
> you work on games / you've been through a launch].
>
> What it is: you type in the positive review rate you'd expect for a game you're working
> on, and it tells you where that number sits among recent Steam releases. Then it lists
> real games at that position so you can look at them.
>
> What it involves: I share a link, you use it while I watch and take notes, and I ask a
> few questions. It's not a test of you — it's a test of whether the thing makes sense.
> About 15 minutes, any time [dates].
>
> Two honest notes:
> • I need people who haven't seen it yet, so please don't look at it beforehand.
> • I'll take notes on what you do and say, but I won't record your name — you'll appear
>   as P1, P2, etc. You can stop at any point, and you don't have to answer anything you
>   don't want to.
>
> Would [date options] work? Happy to work around your schedule.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

---

## If you need a group channel instead

If you have to post to a group chat or forum, add this line, because otherwise volunteers
will pre-read the product and the first-impression evidence is gone:

> If you're interested, please reply and I'll send the link separately — it needs to be
> the first time you see it.

---

## Scheduling target

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Messages sent | 12–15, to individuals |
| Expected conversion | Roughly a third reply and a third of those commit, so expect 5–8 sessions |
| Sessions booked within | 3 days of sending |
| Sessions completed by | 5 days after the first session, leaving time to act on findings |

If fewer than 5 people commit within 3 days, widen the pool rather than lowering the
participant count — the number 5 is the point below which a repeated confusion cannot be
distinguished from a one-off.

---

## Do not

- Do not describe what the answer means before the session. Explaining first destroys the
  comprehension measure.
- Do not recruit people who have already discussed the project with you.
- Do not offer payment you cannot actually make. If you do offer something, record it —
  incentives are a limitation worth stating.
- Do not tell participants what you hope they will say.
